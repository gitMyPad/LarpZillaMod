# LarpZillaMod
A light mod for PvZ Fusion that makes Cherrizilla and Garlizilla as strong as the larped version of them.

**Version 1.0:**

**Cherrizilla Mod features:**

1. Upon gaining 1 Cherrizilla Epic Modifier, Cherrizilla / Garlizilla will gain 5 Devour stacks instead of 1, allowing him to come online much quicker. This also makes it so that Cherrizillas planted after the acquisition of the Epic Modifier (can be either) will immediately gain 35 Devour stacks.

2. Upon gaining 2 Cherrizilla Epic Modifiers, Cherrizilla / Garlizilla will gain 10 Devour stacks instead of 5.

3. Cherrizilla's damage cap is calculated as 1/(3 + n/10) of his max Toughness, with **"n"** corresponding to the number of Devour stacks he currently has. The result of the operation (n/10) is treated as an integer value.

**How to Install**

_Preface:_ This assumes that the user has the multi-language translation version of PvZ Fusion, which comes with MelonLoader.

1. Download the attached dll in the Releases tab.
2. Drag and drop into the Mods folder in your GameInstall folder.
     - Path: <GameInstall>\Mods
3. Enjoy!
